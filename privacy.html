Privacy Policy — Sheet Change Detector

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Sheet Change Detector is a Chrome extension that compares ranges and saved snapshots of Google Sheets spreadsheets and shows what changed, cell by cell.

What the extension accesses

  • Spreadsheet data. With your explicit consent, the extension reads cell values and formulas from the spreadsheets you open, using the Google Sheets API and the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets scope. It writes only when you ask it to save a diff report, and only into a new tab it creates.
  • Snapshot data you create. When you capture a snapshot, that range's values and formulas are stored on your device.

Where your data goes

Nowhere but Google. The extension has no backend server. It does not transmit your spreadsheet data, snapshots, or usage to the developer or to any third party. Requests go directly from your browser to Google's APIs.

What is stored, and where

  • Snapshots, settings and sign-in state are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
  • Uninstalling the extension, or clearing its data in Chrome, deletes everything it stored.

What is not done

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No collection of browsing history, keystrokes, or content from any site other than the Google Sheets pages you use the extension on.

Your control

  • Revoke the extension's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete individual snapshots in the extension, or remove all data by uninstalling it.

Limited Use disclosure

Sheet Change Detector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
