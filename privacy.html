Privacy Policy — Sheet Change Detector

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sheet Change Detector is a Chrome extension that compares ranges and saved snapshots of Google Sheets spreadsheets and shows what changed, cell by cell.

What the extension accesses

  • Spreadsheet data. The extension requests one Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets. It uses the Google Sheets API to read values and, where needed, formulas from ranges you compare or capture, and to look up tab names and IDs. The scope also allows the spreadsheet writes described below.
  • Snapshot data. When you capture a snapshot, or set a range to be captured on a schedule, that range's values and formulas are stored on your device.

What the extension writes

  • The Diff summary tab. After a successful comparison in the panel, the extension creates or updates one rolling Diff summary tab in the open spreadsheet if Generate tab with summary is checked. The option is checked by default; unchecked, the comparison writes no report tabs and leaves existing tabs in place. Changing the option does not rerun a completed comparison. Scheduled comparisons also generate reports, independently of this option. To reuse a report tab, the extension looks for a title such as Diff summary or Diff summary (2) and the Diff report marker in its first cell. It leaves matching titles without that marker untouched and uses an available numbered title if needed. If writing the summary fails, the extension may remove the new summary tab it just created.
  • The Recovery tab. After successfully writing the summary, the extension creates a separate tab if the comparison found at least one removed cell. The tab lists the removed cells and their previous values. It is named Recovery, or Recovery (2), Recovery (3), and so on if the name is taken. The report writer never reuses or updates an existing Recovery tab.
  • Merges you approve. When you apply a merge, the extension writes only approved, writable changes into the target range. Before applying the merge, it rereads the target cells and skips any whose current value differs from the value recorded in the comparison. If you choose Undo apply, the extension writes back the values saved before that merge.
  • Sample tabs. If you choose Create sample tabs, the extension adds two new tabs with sample data to the open spreadsheet. It does not overwrite existing tabs.
  • Clearing generated tabs. When you confirm Clear generated tabs, the extension deletes only the summary tabs it has recorded for the open spreadsheet. It tracks those tabs by ID, so renaming one does not remove it from the list. Recovery tabs, sample tabs, and summary tabs written only by a scheduled comparison are not added to that list.
  • Apart from creating those report and sample tabs, deleting the summary tabs you clear, and the cell writes for merges you approve and for undo, the extension does not edit, rename or delete any other tab.

Where your data goes

The extension sends spreadsheet data directly from your browser to Google's APIs to read ranges and make the spreadsheet writes described above. There is no developer-operated backend and no automatic server copy of your snapshots or comparison history. The extension does not send spreadsheet data, snapshots, comparison history, usage counts or diagnostics to the developer; you can choose to copy and share diagnostics yourself. Its other requests are the ExtensionPay licence and checkout pages and a public tier-limits file, none of which carries spreadsheet data or Google account information. The tier-limits file is requested when you open the panel.

What is stored, and where

  • Snapshots, comparison history, presets, schedules, usage counts, interface settings, diagnostics, and the values needed to undo the last merge are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). The extension also stores a local marker for the last successful sign-in or sign-out, including when it happened. That marker contains no Google token, email address or profile detail.
  • ExtensionPay caches its licence record in Chrome's synced extension storage, falling back to local extension storage if synced storage is unavailable. The record can include the email address you gave ExtensionPay. The extension does not display that address.
  • The extension keeps up to three snapshots per spreadsheet, removing the oldest when a new capture would exceed that limit. Pro comparison history keeps up to 20 entries per spreadsheet. The local diagnostics log also discards older entries as it fills.
  • Uninstalling removes the extension's local data from that Chrome installation. It does not delete report tabs in Google Sheets or records held by the payment providers.

Payments

Pro purchases are handled by ExtensionPay, with Stripe processing payments. When you start a trial or choose to buy Pro, the extension opens an ExtensionPay page in your browser. ExtensionPay receives the email address you provide there, and Stripe processes the card details you enter at checkout; their own privacy policies apply. The extension does not receive or store your card details, and it sends neither provider any Google user data, including spreadsheet contents, snapshots or Google account information.

What is not done

  • The extension does not send analytics or use tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No access to your Google account identity: the extension requests only the spreadsheets scope, so it does not receive your name, email address or profile from Google.
  • No selling of your data.
  • No collection of browsing history, keystrokes, or content from any site other than the Google Sheets pages you use the extension on.

Your control

  • Sign out in the panel to revoke the extension's Google access. To use it with Google Sheets again, sign in and grant access again.
  • Revoke the extension's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete individual snapshots, comparison history entries and presets in the panel. Use Clear generated tabs to remove recorded summaries, or delete report tabs directly in Google Sheets.
  • For data-deletion requests, email theappsdonewell@gmail.com.

Limited Use disclosure

Sheet Change Detector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
