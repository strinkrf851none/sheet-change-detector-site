Sheet Change Detector

See what changed in a Google Sheet — cell by cell, formulas included.

A Chrome extension that opens a side panel on any Google Sheets spreadsheet and compares two tabs, two ranges, or a saved snapshot against the live sheet. Changed cells are highlighted, and a toggle switches the comparison between values and formulas.

  • Cell-by-cell diff with added, removed and changed cells
  • Formula changes, not just number changes
  • Snapshots captured on demand or daily, stored on your device
  • Save the result as a report tab to share with a colleague

The extension has no backend. Spreadsheet data goes only to Google's APIs, and snapshots never leave your browser.
